What the credentials mean

The letters after their names.

Manitoba classifies early childhood staff by training. Here is what each level means for your child's day.

ECE III

Early Childhood Educator III

An ECE II with further post-diploma study or a related degree. Qualified to lead programs and supervise.

ECE II

Early Childhood Educator II

A diploma in early childhood education from an approved program, or its assessed equivalent.

CCA

Child Care Assistant

Works alongside our educators and completes provincially approved training.

Montessori

Montessori diploma

Specialist training in the Montessori method, materials and prepared environment.
