A note from our Director

Every child, known by name.

Each morning I watch children walk into our classrooms and choose their work with real purpose. Some head straight for the Montessori shelves. Others go to the studio to pick up a project they started the day before. That mix of order and creativity is what makes River Heights different.

Our educators are trained in both Montessori and Reggio-inspired practice, and they take the time to know every child and every family. Through our daily e-reports, you will see what your child explored, created and discovered.

We are open all year, so learning never has to pause for the summer. I would love to show you around.

Ms. NatashaDirector, River Heights Montessori
