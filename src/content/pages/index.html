Our approach

Two philosophies. One child.

Most schools choose between structure and creativity. In every River Heights classroom, a Montessori educator and a Reggio-inspired educator work side by side, so children get both.

01 · Montessori

Order, independence, real materials.

Children choose purposeful work and master it at their own pace, guided by a Montessori-trained educator across six learning areas.

02 · Reggio-inspired

A hundred languages of learning.

Art, music, drama and building become the ways children explore their ideas. Long projects grow from their own questions and are documented as they unfold.

03 · STEAM

Curiosity, every single day.

Science, technology, engineering, art and math are woven through the day in play, books and conversation, starting with our youngest learners.
